04 / The screening gate

Three steps before a record opens

Every activity that collects health information runs the same sequence, so a resident always knows what was asked and why.

  1. 01

    Watch

    A two-minute briefing on what the screening covers, played in the local language.

    2 min
  2. 02

    Survey

    Eleven questions, answered with a facilitator present. Nothing is stored until it is read back.

    11 items
  3. 03

    Unlock

    The record opens to the resident first, then to the organisation they name.

    Consent held
